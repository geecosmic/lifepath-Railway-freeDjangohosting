How to Calculate

Add together the digits of your birth date (month, day, and year) and reduce the sum to a single digit or a master number (11 or 22). For example, if your birthday is August 18, 1989: month (8) + day (1+8=9) + year (1+9+8+9=27 → 2+7=9). Then 8 + 9 + 9 = 26 → 2 + 6 = 8.
Add the day and month of your birth, then reduce to a single digit between 1 and 9 (master numbers 11 and 22 are not reduced). Example: born August 5 → 8 + 5 = 13 → 1 + 3 = 4.
Sum your birth day, birth month, and the current year. Then reduce the total to a single digit. Example: birthday July 15, calculating for 2024 → 15 + 7 + 2024 = 2046 → 2 + 0 + 4 + 6 = 12 → 1 + 2 = 3.
Sum the reduced month digit and your personal year number. Example: personal year is 9 and February is the month → 9 + 2 = 11 → 1 + 1 = 2.
Add the number of the day to your personal month number. Reduce to a single digit if needed.
