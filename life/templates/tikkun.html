Tikkun Hanefesh

Historically, kabbalists have practiced this healing meditation (Tikkun Hanefesh) twice daily, seven days a week, to rejuvenate and restore all body organs.

Known as Tikkun Hanefesh, or the Correction of the Soul, it is one of the most potent healing methods in Kabbalah. Using the chart as a reference, place your right hand over the specific part of the body where you wish to channel energy. The body parts are listed, and each box is numbered for easy navigation, starting from box #1 (the skull — keter) and continuing to box #17 (the feet — malchut).

Focus on the Aramaic letter combination corresponding to the area of the body you are concentrating on, and visualize Light flowing through your right hand into that part.

The Tikkun HaNefesh is used for healing and is a powerful tool for regeneration and revitalization of the body's organs. It promotes a deeper connection to divine attributes like compassion and patience, potentially leading to holistic well-being.

The Prayer of the Kabbalist

Ana BeKo'ach

The Ana beKo'ach, often referred to as the 42-letter Name of God, is a profound and mystical formula that holds significant importance in Kabbalistic tradition.

This unique composition consists of 42 letters meticulously arranged into seven distinct sentences, with each sentence containing six words. This structured format is not merely for aesthetic purposes; it carries deep spiritual meaning. Each of the seven sentences corresponds to various elements of the cosmos, including the seven days of the week, seven specific angels, and a particular heavenly body, thereby linking the microcosm of human experience with the macrocosm of the universe.

The origins of the letters that form the Ana beKo'ach can be traced back to the first 42 letters of the Book of Genesis, the foundational text of the Hebrew Bible. Kabbalists believe that this connection is not coincidental; rather, it signifies a direct link to the very moment of Creation itself.

When practitioners engage in the Ana beKo'ach meditation, they are not merely reciting words; they are embarking on a spiritual journey that allows them to reconnect with this primordial energy.

The transformative power of the Ana beKo'ach meditation lies in its ability to elevate the practitioner's consciousness, aligning them with the divine flow of the universe.

The 72 Names of God

Scan and read the names from right to left

In the Zohar, the ancient Kabbalist Rav Shimon bar Yochai posits that it was Moses, rather than God, who parted the Red Sea, facilitating the Israelites' escape from Pharaoh and the Egyptian army. To achieve this miraculous feat, Moses harnessed the power of certainty along with an advanced spiritual technology.

For the past 2000 years, this formula, hidden within the Zohar, has remained undisclosed. This formula is 72 sequences made up of Hebrew letters that hold remarkable power, enabling the transcendence of the laws of nature, including those governing human behavior.

Though the formula is embedded within the Biblical narrative of the parting of the Red Sea, it remained a secret known only to a select group of kabbalists. These kabbalists understood that one day, the formula would be unveiled to the world at the right moment.

Today, after approximately 2,000 years of obscurity, modern seekers have the opportunity to access this power and energy by learning about and invoking the 72 Names of God. The 72 Names consist of three-letter sequences that function as an index to specific spiritual frequencies. By simply observing the letters and closing your eyes to visualize them, you can establish a connection with these potent frequencies.
